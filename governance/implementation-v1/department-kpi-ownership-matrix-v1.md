## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the company-wide ownership structure for key performance indicators (KPIs).

The objective is to ensure that every critical business metric has:

- One accountable department
- A defined KPI
- A measurable target
- A verified data source
- A reporting frequency
- Clear escalation responsibility

This matrix connects the Company KPI & OKR Framework v1 with individual departments.

---

## 2. KPI Governance Principles

Every KPI must have:

1. KPI name
2. KPI definition
3. Accountable department
4. KPI owner
5. Baseline
6. Target
7. Deadline
8. Data source
9. Reporting frequency
10. Status
11. Escalation threshold

### Data Integrity Rule

No KPI baseline or performance value may be invented.

If verified data is unavailable, the value must be recorded as:

`TBD`

or

`N/A`

# 6. KPI Status Definitions

| Status | Definition |
|---|---|
| 🟢 On Track | KPI is meeting or exceeding approved target |
| 🟡 At Risk | KPI is below target but recoverable |
| 🔴 Off Track | KPI materially below target |
| ⚪ TBD | Baseline or target has not yet been verified |
| 🔵 Not Applicable | KPI does not currently apply |

---

# 7. Escalation Rules

### 🟢 On Track
Department continues normal execution.

### 🟡 At Risk
Department Head submits corrective action within the next reporting cycle.

### 🔴 Off Track
Department Head submits:

- Root cause
- Corrective action
- Required resources
- Revised forecast
- Recovery deadline

CEO HQ reviews material issues.

### Critical Risk
Any KPI issue involving:

- Material financial loss
- Legal/compliance exposure
- Major quality failure
- Customer safety
- Cybersecurity
- Severe supply disruption
- Material reputational damage

must be escalated to CEO HQ immediately.

# 9. Data Governance

All KPI data must originate from an identifiable source.

Acceptable sources may include:

- Accounting system
- E-commerce platform
- ERP
- CRM
- Manufacturing records
- Inventory system
- Logistics records
- Customer service platform
- HR system
- Analytics platform
- Approved operational spreadsheets

Manual estimates must be explicitly identified and must not be presented as verified performance.

---

# 10. Target Governance

Targets must be approved only after baseline validation.

Target-setting factors:

- Historical performance
- Current capacity
- Market conditions
- Financial constraints
- Customer expectations
- Strategic priorities
- Operational feasibility
- Risk exposure

---

# 11. Review Cadence

### Weekly CEO Review

Review critical operating KPIs and red/amber items.

### Monthly Executive Review

Review all departmental KPIs, budget performance, risks, and corrective actions.

### Quarterly Strategic Review

Review:

- OKR achievement
- Strategic progress
- Department performance
- Resource allocation
- Target revisions
- New strategic initiatives

---

# 12. Accountability Principle

Each KPI must have **one accountable department**.

Supporting departments may contribute data or execution, but accountability cannot be ambiguous.

CEO HQ retains final executive oversight.
