# Human Resources Baseline v1

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the controlled operating baseline for Human Resources at Vybe Collections.

The objective is to build and maintain a capable, productive, ethical, compliant, and scalable workforce aligned with company strategy while protecting employees, management, and the company.

---

## 2. Objectives

The department shall:

- Plan workforce requirements.
- Define roles and responsibilities.
- Recruit qualified employees.
- Maintain consistent hiring standards.
- Support structured onboarding.
- Establish performance-management practices.
- Coordinate employee development and training.
- Maintain appropriate compensation governance.
- Protect employee information.
- Maintain accurate employee records.
- Support workplace compliance.
- Manage employee lifecycle processes.
- Provide workforce analytics to executive management.

---

## 3. Scope

This baseline covers:

**Workforce Planning → Recruitment → Hiring → Onboarding → Roles & Responsibilities → Performance Management → Training → Employee KPIs → Compensation Governance → Employee Records → HR Compliance → Offboarding**

It applies to employees and, where relevant, contractors, temporary workers, consultants, and other workforce participants subject to applicable company policies and legal requirements.

# 5. Workforce Planning

Workforce planning should align employee capacity with:

- Company strategy
- Department objectives
- Revenue requirements
- Production requirements
- Customer demand
- Technology requirements
- Geographic expansion
- Financial capacity

Workforce requests should identify:

- Department
- Role
- Business need
- Responsibilities
- Required skills
- Expected workload
- Employment type
- Proposed timing
- Budget impact

New positions should follow applicable approval requirements.

---

# 6. Organization Structure

The company should maintain a current organization structure showing:

- Departments
- Leadership
- Reporting relationships
- Key roles
- Functional responsibilities
- Decision ownership

Organizational changes should be documented.

---

# 7. Job Descriptions

Each approved role should have a documented job description containing, as appropriate:

- Job title
- Department
- Reporting relationship
- Role purpose
- Responsibilities
- Required qualifications
- Required skills
- Experience requirements
- Decision authority
- Key performance indicators

Job descriptions should be reviewed periodically.

---

# 8. Recruitment

Recruitment should follow a controlled process:

**Workforce Need → Position Approval → Job Description → Candidate Sourcing → Screening → Interview → Evaluation → Selection → Approval → Offer → Onboarding**

Recruitment criteria should be relevant to the role.

Hiring decisions should not be based on unlawful discriminatory criteria.

---

# 9. Candidate Screening

Candidate screening may consider:

- Qualifications
- Relevant experience
- Technical skills
- Functional capabilities
- Role-specific competencies
- Behavioral competencies
- Availability
- Compensation expectations where appropriate
- Other lawful job requirements

Candidate information must be handled appropriately.

---

# 10. Interview Process

Interviews should use structured evaluation where practical.

Evaluation should be based on:

- Role requirements
- Competencies
- Relevant experience
- Problem-solving capability
- Technical capability
- Communication
- Cultural and organizational alignment

Interview decisions should be documented sufficiently to support consistent hiring.

---

# 11. Hiring Approval

Before a hiring offer is issued, appropriate approvals should confirm:

- Position is authorized.
- Budget is available.
- Compensation is within approved parameters.
- Candidate meets required criteria.
- Employment terms are appropriate.
- Applicable compliance requirements are addressed.

---

# 12. Employment Offers

Employment offers should accurately document applicable:

- Position
- Compensation
- Employment type
- Start date
- Reporting relationship
- Working arrangements
- Applicable benefits
- Conditions of employment

Offers must comply with applicable law and company policy.

---

# 13. Onboarding

The onboarding process should establish:

**Offer Acceptance → Documentation → System Access → Orientation → Role Training → Team Integration → Initial Objectives → Review**

New employees should receive relevant information regarding:

- Company mission
- Policies
- Role expectations
- Department responsibilities
- Security
- Workplace standards
- Performance expectations
- Applicable tools and systems

---

# 14. Employee Access

Employee access to systems and information should follow:

- Role requirements
- Least-privilege principles
- Approval controls
- Security requirements

Access should be removed or modified promptly when employment or responsibilities change.

---

# 15. Probation / Initial Review

Where applicable, new employees may have an initial evaluation period.

Review should consider:

- Role performance
- Reliability
- Skills
- Conduct
- Learning progress
- Team integration
- Achievement of initial objectives

Applicable employment requirements must be followed.

---

# 16. Performance Management

Performance management should establish a consistent cycle:

**Objectives → Performance → Review → Feedback → Development → Improvement**

Employees should understand:

- Role expectations
- Performance standards
- KPIs
- Objectives
- Review frequency
- Development expectations

---

Role-specific KPIs should be aligned with:

- Department objectives
- Company strategy
- Role responsibilities
- Measurable outcomes

Examples may include:

- Productivity
- Quality
- Timeliness
- Revenue contribution
- Customer outcomes
- Project completion
- Operational efficiency
- Error rates

KPIs must be relevant to the employee's actual responsibilities.

---

# 18. Performance Reviews

Performance reviews should evaluate:

- Objective achievement
- KPI performance
- Quality of work
- Productivity
- Collaboration
- Reliability
- Skills
- Development progress
- Future objectives

Performance records should be maintained appropriately.

---

# 19. Performance Improvement

Where performance does not meet expectations, the company may establish a structured improvement process.

An improvement plan may include:

- Performance gap
- Expected standard
- Required actions
- Support provided
- Review period
- Measurement
- Follow-up

Performance management must be fair, documented, and consistent with applicable requirements.

- Role-specific skills

- Operational procedures
- Technology

- Compliance
- Workplace policies

- Leadership development

Compensation should be managed through controlled processes.

Compensation decisions should consider:

- Role requirements
- Market conditions
- Employee capability
- Performance
- Internal consistency
- Budget

- Leave records

- Data minimization

- Authorized use
- Appropriate retention

- Safety concerns

# 39. Workforce Risks

The department shall monitor:

### Talent Risk
The company cannot attract or retain required capabilities.

### Capacity Risk
Workforce capacity is insufficient for business requirements.

### Performance Risk
Employees or teams consistently fail to achieve required standards.

### Compliance Risk
Employment practices fail applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

### Data Risk
Employee information is improperly accessed or disclosed.

### Culture Risk
Poor workplace practices damage productivity or reputation.

### Leadership Risk
Insufficient leadership capability limits organizational growth.

### Cost Risk
Workforce costs exceed sustainable financial capacity.

### Continuity Risk
Critical knowledge or roles depend excessively on individual employees.

---

- Workforce planning

# 43. Evidence Standard

HR records should distinguish between:

- Proposed
- Requested
- Approved
- Active
- Completed
- Suspended
- Terminated
- Archived
