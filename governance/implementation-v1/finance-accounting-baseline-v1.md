## Data Integrity

- No numerical values are estimated or fabricated.
- Missing information is recorded as **TBD**.
- Every numerical baseline must have an identifiable source.
- The reporting period must be explicitly confirmed.
- Calculations must follow the approved KPI definitions.
- Evidence must be retained for every verified numerical baseline.

## CEO HQ Verification

**Status: Pending departmental submission and evidence review.**

A KPI becomes **Verified** only when:

1. The source is identifiable.
2. The reporting period is confirmed.
3. The calculation is validated.
4. The responsible department confirms the value.
5. Supporting evidence is available.

## Target Approval Gate

Proposed targets remain **Proposed** until CEO HQ reviews:

- Verified baseline performance
- Historical trend
- Production and operational capacity
- Financial implications
- Customer impact
- Strategic relevance
- Cross-department dependencies
