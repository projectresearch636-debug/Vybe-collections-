# Evidence and Verification Policy

## Purpose

Prevent unsupported claims of successful execution.

## Status Definitions

### PLANNED

The change has been designed but not implemented.

### CONFIGURED

The required configuration has been created.

### AUTHORIZED

The required account or integration permissions have been granted.

### EXECUTED

The operation was actually performed.

### VERIFIED

The operation succeeded and evidence confirms the result.

### BLOCKED

A dependency, authorization issue, or other condition prevents execution.

## Verification Standard

Permissions, configuration, repository ownership, or intended architecture do not by themselves prove execution.

Where applicable, verification requires a successful controlled transaction and appropriate evidence.

## Reporting

Technical reports must identify:

- What was requested
- What was changed
- What was executed
- What evidence exists
- What remains blocked
- Remaining risks
- Required next actions

## Prohibition

Never fabricate execution results or represent an unverified operation as verified.
