# Market Research & Business Intelligence Baseline v1

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the controlled operating baseline for Market Research & Business Intelligence at Vybe Collections.

The objective is to provide reliable external market intelligence, customer insight, competitive intelligence, trend intelligence, pricing intelligence, demand signals, and strategic research that support evidence-based business decisions.

---

## 2. Objectives

The department shall:

- Understand target customers and their evolving needs.
- Monitor market size, growth, and structural changes.
- Track competitors and competitive positioning.
- Identify fashion, consumer, cultural, and category trends.
- Evaluate product and category opportunities.
- Support pricing intelligence.
- Identify demand signals.
- Evaluate geographic opportunities.
- Support brand, product, marketing, sales, retail, and expansion decisions.
- Establish research quality and evidence standards.
- Maintain a structured market-intelligence repository.
- Convert external information into actionable strategic insights.
- Measure research effectiveness.

---

## 3. Scope

This baseline covers:

**Market Intelligence → Customer Research → Competitor Analysis → Fashion & Consumer Trends → Market Sizing → Product Intelligence → Pricing Intelligence → Demand Signals → Geographic Analysis → Strategic Insights → Research KPIs**

It applies to research concerning:

- Customers

# 6. Research Planning

Research initiatives should identify:

- Business question
- Decision to be supported
- Research objective
- Target audience or market
- Required information
- Research methodology
- Data sources
- Timeline
- Owner
- Expected business impact

Research without a defined business purpose should be appropriately prioritized or deferred.

---

# 7. Research Prioritization

Research requests may be classified as:

### Critical

Required for an immediate major business, financial, customer, legal, or strategic decision.

### High

Material strategic or commercial decision support.

### Standard

Routine market or customer intelligence.

### Low

Exploratory or non-urgent research.

Priority should consider business impact, urgency, evidence availability, cost, and decision value.

# 11. Evidence Hierarchy

Research evidence should be assessed according to:

1. Direct primary evidence
2. High-quality authoritative data
3. Reputable industry research
4. Reliable secondary sources
5. Public market observations
6. Unverified commentary or anecdotal information

Lower-quality evidence should not be presented as equivalent to validated research.

# 22. Trend Lifecycle

Trends may be classified as:

### Emerging

Early signal with limited evidence.

### Growing

Increasing evidence across multiple sources.

### Established

Strong evidence of sustained adoption.

### Declining

Evidence indicates decreasing relevance.

### Saturated

High adoption with reduced differentiation potential.

# 58. Research Risk Management

Key risks include:

### Evidence Risk

Weak or unreliable evidence produces poor decisions.

### Research Bias Risk

Biased methodology produces misleading conclusions.

### Market Forecast Risk

Market conditions change faster than forecasts.

### Competitive Intelligence Risk

Incomplete information creates an inaccurate competitive picture.

### Privacy Risk

Customer or participant information is improperly handled.

### AI Research Risk

AI-generated information contains unsupported or fabricated claims.

### Strategic Interpretation Risk

Correct research is incorrectly interpreted or applied.

---

# 59. Risk Mitigation

Mitigation includes:

- Source validation
- Methodological review
- Multiple-source triangulation
- Research documentation
- Privacy controls
- Human review
- Evidence classification
- Periodic research updates
- Cross-functional review

Important market intelligence should be refreshed according to its rate of change.

# 62. Market Research vs. Data Analytics

The functions shall remain distinct:

### Market Research & Business Intelligence

Primary focus:

**External information → customers → competitors → trends → markets → opportunities**

### Data Analytics & Business Intelligence

Primary focus:

**Internal business data → KPIs → performance → forecasting → analytics → decision support**

Both departments should collaborate when external and internal evidence must be combined.
