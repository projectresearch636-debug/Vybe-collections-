# Legal & Compliance Baseline v1

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the controlled operating baseline for Legal & Compliance at Vybe Collections.

The objective is to ensure that company activities are conducted in accordance with applicable laws, regulations, contractual obligations, internal policies, and ethical standards while reducing legal, regulatory, financial, operational, and reputational risk.

---

## 2. Objectives

The department shall:

- Establish legal and compliance governance.
- Identify applicable legal and regulatory requirements.
- Establish contract review and approval controls.
- Protect company intellectual property.
- Protect customer and employee information.
- Support consumer-protection compliance.
- Support employment compliance.
- Establish advertising and marketing compliance controls.
- Monitor vendor and supplier compliance.
- Manage legal incidents and disputes.
- Maintain appropriate legal and compliance records.
- Escalate material legal and regulatory risks.
- Support responsible and sustainable business growth.

---

## 3. Scope

This baseline covers:

**Legal Governance → Contracts → Regulatory Compliance → Consumer Protection → Employment Compliance → Intellectual Property → Privacy → Advertising Compliance → Vendor Compliance → Risk & Incident Escalation → Records**

It applies across all departments, subsidiaries, operating locations, vendors, contractors, business partners, and other relevant third parties to the extent applicable.

# 5. Legal Governance

Legal & Compliance should maintain a documented framework defining:

- Legal ownership
- Compliance ownership
- Approval authority
- Legal review requirements
- Escalation procedures
- Recordkeeping requirements
- External legal counsel engagement where necessary

Departments must escalate material legal questions rather than making unsupported legal determinations independently.

---

# 6. Legal Review Triggers

Legal & Compliance review should be considered before material commitments involving:

- New contracts
- Major commercial agreements
- Employment agreements where legal review is required
- Intellectual property
- Data processing
- Advertising claims
- Regulatory requirements
- Product compliance
- Significant disputes
- New markets
- New business models
- High-risk vendors
- Material customer terms

---

# 7. Contract Governance

Contracts should follow a controlled lifecycle:

**Request → Business Review → Legal Review → Commercial Approval → Authorized Execution → Repository → Obligation Monitoring → Renewal/Termination**

Contracts must not be executed by unauthorized personnel.

---

# 8. Contract Requests

Contract requests should identify:

- Counterparty
- Business purpose
- Scope
- Commercial value
- Term
- Key obligations
- Risks
- Required approvals
- Relevant departments
- Proposed effective date

Incomplete requests may require clarification before legal review.

---

# 9. Contract Review

Contract review may assess:

- Parties
- Scope
- Pricing
- Payment
- Term
- Renewal
- Termination
- Liability
- Indemnification
- Confidentiality
- Intellectual property
- Data protection
- Warranties
- Dispute resolution
- Governing law
- Compliance obligations

Review requirements should be proportional to risk.

---

# 10. Contract Approval

Contracts should receive appropriate approvals based on:

- Financial value
- Legal risk
- Strategic importance
- Contract duration
- Liability exposure
- Data access
- Intellectual property
- Business impact

Approval authority must be clearly defined.

---

# 11. Contract Execution

Only authorized representatives may execute company contracts.

Signed contracts should be stored in an approved repository.

Execution records should include:

- Final agreement
- Signatures
- Effective date
- Expiration date
- Renewal information
- Relevant amendments

---

# 12. Contract Obligations

Relevant departments should monitor contractual obligations including:

- Payments
- Deliverables
- Service levels
- Reporting
- Certifications
- Renewal dates
- Termination notices
- Compliance requirements

Material missed obligations should be escalated.

---

# 13. Contract Renewals

Contract renewal dates should be monitored.

Where practical, renewal review should begin sufficiently before expiration to allow:

- Commercial evaluation
- Legal review
- Performance assessment
- Renegotiation
- Termination planning

Automatic renewals should not be allowed to occur without appropriate oversight where material.

---

The company should identify legal and regulatory requirements relevant to its activities and operating markets.

Compliance areas may include:

- Corporate requirements

- Product requirements

- Tax
- Privacy
- Intellectual property
- E-commerce

- Workplace safety

- Responsible department

- Evidence requirement

- Escalation process

No compliance requirement should remain without an accountable owner where practical.

- Training

- Assessed

- Investigated

# 43. Risk Classification

Legal and compliance risks may be categorized as:

### Low
Limited impact and manageable through routine controls.

### Moderate
Requires departmental management and documented mitigation.

### High
Potential material financial, regulatory, operational, or reputational impact.

### Critical
Potential severe legal, regulatory, financial, safety, or reputation consequences requiring immediate executive attention.

---

# 44. Risk Mitigation

Legal and compliance risks should be managed through:

- Preventive controls
- Legal review
- Contract controls
- Compliance monitoring
- Training
- Documentation
- Vendor due diligence
- Privacy controls
- IP protection
- Incident response
- Corrective actions

---

# 45. Compliance Exceptions

Exceptions to established legal or compliance controls should:

- Be documented
- Identify the reason
- Identify the responsible authority
- Assess risk
- Establish mitigation
- Have an appropriate expiration or review date where practical

No exception should be treated as permanent without appropriate governance.

# 50. Evidence Standard

Legal and compliance records should distinguish between:

- Identified
- Under Review
- Approved
- Compliant
- Non-Compliant
- Remediation Required
- Escalated
- Resolved
- Closed

Legal or compliance status must be based on appropriate evidence.

Unverified legal conclusions must not be represented as confirmed facts.
