# Retail & International Expansion Baseline v1

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the controlled operating baseline for Retail & International Expansion at Vybe Collections.

The objective is to provide a disciplined framework for physical retail, market-entry evaluation, international expansion, location strategy, channel development, operational readiness, customer experience, regulatory coordination, financial evaluation, and expansion risk management.

This baseline establishes internal governance and does not constitute jurisdiction-specific legal, tax, regulatory, immigration, employment, or financial advice.

---

## 2. Objectives

Retail & International Expansion shall:

- Evaluate new markets systematically.
- Establish disciplined market-entry criteria.
- Evaluate retail opportunities.
- Protect brand consistency across markets.
- Assess financial viability before expansion.
- Coordinate regulatory and legal requirements.
- Establish operational readiness.
- Manage international expansion risks.
- Develop scalable retail and distribution models.
- Monitor market performance.
- Establish measurable expansion KPIs.

---

## 3. Scope

This baseline covers:

**Expansion Strategy → Market Selection → Market Research → Entry Evaluation → Retail Strategy → Location Strategy → Channel Strategy → Financial Feasibility → Legal & Compliance → Supply Chain → Staffing → Technology → Customer Experience → Launch → Performance Monitoring → International Risk → Expansion KPIs**

## 4. Expansion Governance

All material expansion proposals should be evaluated through a controlled process:

**Opportunity Identification → Market Assessment → Business Case → Risk Review → Executive Approval → Implementation → Launch → Performance Review**

No material expansion should proceed without appropriate executive approval.
