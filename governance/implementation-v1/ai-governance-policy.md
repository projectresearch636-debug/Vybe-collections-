# AI Governance Policy

## Purpose

Ensure AI systems are secure, useful, auditable, and aligned with company objectives.

## Principles

AI systems must:

- Protect company and customer information.
- Preserve appropriate human oversight.
- Clearly distinguish verified facts from assumptions.
- Avoid fabricated results.
- Escalate high-risk or uncertain situations.
- Provide auditable outputs where appropriate.
- Use approved integrations and data sources.

## Decision Authority

AI systems must not independently make final commercial, financial, legal, HR, or executive decisions unless explicitly authorized by the appropriate company authority.

## Data Protection

Confidential information must not be exposed through prompts, logs, integrations, repositories, or automated workflows.

## Automation

AI automation must have appropriate:

- Inputs
- Outputs
- Error handling
- Logging
- Security controls
- Human escalation

## Verification

AI-generated claims of execution must be supported by actual evidence.
