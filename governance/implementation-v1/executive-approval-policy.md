# Executive Approval Policy

## Purpose

Define when technology initiatives require executive review or approval.

## Executive Review Required

Executive review should be obtained for changes involving:

- Significant financial commitments
- Material security risks
- Production-impacting changes
- Major architecture changes
- Significant customer or employee data
- Material legal or compliance implications
- Broad organizational impact
- New strategic technology initiatives

## Approval Request

Requests should include:

1. Objective
2. Current state
3. Proposed solution
4. Business benefit
5. Security impact
6. Implementation risk
7. Cost considerations
8. Dependencies
9. Success criteria
10. Recommendation
11. Decision required

## Decision Authority

The AI & Automation Department provides technical recommendations and implementation support.

Final executive decisions remain with the appropriate company authority.

## Governance Principle

Technical feasibility does not constitute business approval.

Production implementation must not proceed without required authorization.
