---

## 1. Purpose

This Executive Action Register converts the Vybe Collections CEO Headquarters Master Plan v1.0 into an executable company-wide control structure.

It serves as the tracking layer between:

**Strategic Plan → Department Execution → KPI Measurement → CEO Review**

No major initiative should proceed to production execution without an accountable owner, measurable outcome, defined dependencies, identified risks, and appropriate executive approval.

---

## 2. Governance Rule

All material actions must be:

- Aligned with the CEO Headquarters Master Plan.
- Assigned to an accountable department.
- Given a defined priority.
- Associated with measurable KPIs.
- Assigned a target date.
- Reviewed for dependencies and risks.
- Recorded with an executive decision status.
- Executed through the appropriate departmental and GitHub governance workflow.

Changes affecting protected production branches must follow the repository's established pull-request and validation requirements.

## 5. Information Required Before Final Approval

The current Master Plan summary identifies an Immediate Action Register but does not provide the complete underlying action-level dataset.

Therefore, the actions above constitute an initial controlled execution scaffold and must not be interpreted as historical entries from the original Master Plan.

Before final approval, CEO Headquarters must validate:

1. Department ownership.
2. Priority classification.
3. KPI definitions.
4. KPI targets.
5. Target dates.
6. Dependencies.
7. Risk ratings.
8. Risk mitigation plans.
9. Budget requirements.
10. Executive decision status.

---

## 6. Cross-Department Coordination

Each action must be reviewed for impacts on other departments.

Examples include:

- Product development ↔ Textile & Fabric Sourcing ↔ Manufacturing.
- Manufacturing ↔ Quality Assurance ↔ Supply Chain.
- Marketing ↔ Sales ↔ E-commerce ↔ Customer Service.
- Finance ↔ Procurement ↔ Manufacturing ↔ International Expansion.
- IT & AI ↔ Legal & Compliance ↔ every department using AI or automation.
- Data Analytics ↔ all departments providing or consuming KPI data.

No department should optimize its own KPI at the expense of company-wide objectives.

---

## 7. KPI Governance

Company KPIs should be tracked through the CEO Headquarters dashboard.

Core KPI categories include:

- Revenue
- Gross margin
- Net profit
- Cash position
- Inventory turnover
- Manufacturing efficiency
- Product defect rate
- Customer satisfaction
- Customer retention
- Website conversion rate
- Marketing ROI
- Sales growth
- Employee productivity
- On-time delivery
- Return rate

Where data is unavailable, the responsible department must define the measurement method before a target is approved.

---

## 8. Risk Management

Every P0 and P1 action must identify:

- Risk owner
- Risk probability
- Risk impact
- Mitigation
- Contingency
- Monitoring KPI
- Escalation trigger

Material risks must be escalated to CEO Headquarters.

---

## 9. Change Control

Any material change to this register must be submitted through the approved GitHub pull-request workflow.

Changes to protected production branches must satisfy all required repository governance and validation checks.

Direct unauthorized modification of protected production branches is prohibited.

---

## 10. Approval Status

**Executive Action Register v1:** Draft

**CEO Headquarters Status:** Pending final review

**Production Execution:** Not automatically approved

**GitHub Production Merge:** Requires normal governance workflow and required validation checks

## 11. Review Cadence

The Executive Action Register should be reviewed:

- **Weekly:** execution progress and blockers
- **Monthly:** KPI and priority review
- **Quarterly:** strategic alignment review
- **Annually:** full Master Plan and action-register review
