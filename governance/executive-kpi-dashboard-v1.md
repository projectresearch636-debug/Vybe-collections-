## 1. Purpose

The Executive KPI Dashboard provides CEO Headquarters with a consolidated view of company performance across all departments.

The dashboard connects:

**Company Strategy → Department Objectives → KPIs → Performance → Risks → Corrective Actions → Executive Decisions**

The dashboard is intended to support:

- Executive decision-making
- Company-wide performance monitoring
- Early risk identification
- Cross-department coordination
- Resource allocation
- Corrective action
- Strategic planning
- Quarterly business reviews

No performance value may be invented.

# 4. KPI Status Definitions

| Status | Definition |
|---|---|
| 🟢 On Track | KPI is meeting or exceeding approved target |
| 🟡 At Risk | KPI is below target or trending negatively |
| 🔴 Critical | KPI requires immediate corrective action |
| ⚪ TBD | Verified data is not yet available |
| 🔵 Complete | Reporting period has been closed and validated |
| ⚫ Escalated | CEO Headquarters intervention required |

Status must be determined using approved KPI definitions and verified data.

# 21. KPI Review Process

The Executive KPI review process shall follow:

1. Collect department KPI data.
2. Validate data.
3. Compare actual performance against approved targets.
4. Identify positive and negative variance.
5. Identify trends.
6. Identify cross-department causes.
7. Identify material risks.
8. Create corrective actions where required.
9. Assign accountable owners.
10. Set deadlines.
11. Escalate material issues.
12. Review corrective-action completion.

# 25. KPI Ownership Principle

Every KPI must have:

- One primary accountable department.
- A defined KPI definition.
- A measurement frequency.
- A baseline.
- A target.
- An actual value.
- A status.
- A data source.
- A reporting owner.

Supporting departments may contribute data or execution but do not replace primary accountability.

# 28. Dashboard Governance

Data Analytics & Business Intelligence shall coordinate KPI data consolidation.

Department owners remain responsible for the accuracy of their submitted operational data.

Finance remains the authority for financial reporting.

CEO Headquarters remains the final executive authority for company-level interpretation and decision-making.
