## 1. Purpose

This register establishes the operational linking and accountability structure across all departments of Vybe Collections.

The objective is to ensure that:

- Every department has a defined role.
- Cross-department dependencies are documented.
- Material deliverables have accountable owners.
- Required inputs and outputs are defined.
- Deadlines are tracked.
- Dependency status is visible.
- Escalation paths are established.
- Department execution remains aligned with company strategy.
- KPI ownership is connected to operational execution.

This register complements:

- Department KPI Ownership Matrix v1
- Company-Wide Governance Integration v1
- Company KPI & OKR Framework
- Department baseline documents

---

## 2. Operating Model

Vybe Collections shall operate as one integrated organization.

The company operating model is:

**Strategy → Departments → Objectives → KPIs → Dependencies → Execution → Measurement → Review → Corrective Action**

CEO Headquarters maintains company-wide coordination, prioritization, executive oversight, and final decision authority.

Departments remain accountable for execution within their approved areas of responsibility.

## 4. Accountability Rules

Every material cross-department dependency must identify:

1. Requesting department
2. Supporting department
3. Deliverable
4. Accountable owner
5. Required input
6. Deadline
7. Dependency status
8. KPI linkage
9. Escalation path
10. Success criteria

No material dependency may remain without an accountable owner.

Supporting departments provide inputs and execution support but do not automatically assume accountability for the primary outcome.

# 6. Product Lifecycle Integration

The primary product lifecycle is:

**Market Research → Brand Strategy → Product Development → Textile & Fabric Sourcing → Procurement → Manufacturing → Quality Assurance → Finance → Marketing → Sales → E-commerce → Customer Service → Product Improvement**

### 6.1 Market Research → Brand

**Input:** Market trends, customer insights, competitor intelligence

**Output:** Approved market opportunity and strategic insight

**Accountable:** Market Research & Business Intelligence

**Supporting:** Brand Strategy & Creative

**KPI linkage:**

- Market research completion
- Competitive intelligence coverage
- Market insight adoption
- Forecast accuracy

---

### 6.2 Brand → Product Development

**Input:** Brand positioning, customer profile, creative direction

**Output:** Approved product direction

**Accountable:** Brand Strategy & Creative

**Supporting:** Fashion Design & Product Development

**KPI linkage:**

- Brand guideline compliance
- Creative delivery rate
- Product launch readiness

---

### 6.3 Product Development → Sourcing

**Input:** Product specifications and material requirements

**Output:** Approved material requirements

**Accountable:** Fashion Design & Product Development

**Supporting:** Textile & Fabric Sourcing

**KPI linkage:**

- Product development cycle time
- Sample approval rate
- Product launch readiness

---

### 6.4 Sourcing → Procurement

**Input:** Material specifications and approved supplier requirements

**Output:** Qualified supplier and purchasing requirement

**Accountable:** Textile & Fabric Sourcing

**Supporting:** Procurement & Vendor Management

**KPI linkage:**

- Supplier quality rate
- Approved supplier coverage
- Sourcing cost variance

---

### 6.5 Procurement → Manufacturing

**Input:** Approved purchase commitments and material availability

**Output:** Required production inputs

**Accountable:** Procurement & Vendor Management

**Supporting:** Manufacturing & Production

**KPI linkage:**

- Purchase cost variance
- Supplier on-time performance
- Contract compliance

---

### 6.6 Manufacturing → Quality Assurance

**Input:** Production output

**Output:** Quality inspection results and corrective actions

**Accountable:** Manufacturing & Production for production execution

**Accountable quality authority:** Quality Assurance

**KPI linkage:**

- Production efficiency
- Production on-time rate
- First-pass yield
- Defect rate

---

### 6.7 Quality Assurance → Supply Chain

**Input:** Approved finished goods

**Output:** Quality-released inventory

**Accountable:** Quality Assurance

**Supporting:** Supply Chain & Logistics

**KPI linkage:**

- Defect rate
- First-pass quality rate
- Corrective action closure

---

### 6.8 Supply Chain → Sales

**Input:** Inventory availability and delivery information

**Output:** Reliable product availability

**Accountable:** Supply Chain & Logistics

**Supporting:** Sales & Business Development

**KPI linkage:**

- Inventory turnover
- Stockout rate
- On-time delivery

**Customer Service → Data Analytics → Market Research → Product Development → Quality → Brand → Marketing → Sales → E-commerce**

# 15. Dependency Status Definitions

| Status | Definition |
|---|---|
| 🟢 On Track | Dependency progressing according to plan |
| 🟡 At Risk | Dependency may miss requirements or deadline |
| 🔴 Off Track | Dependency materially failing |
| ⚪ Not Started | Dependency has not started |
| 🔵 Complete | Dependency successfully delivered |
| ⚫ Escalated | CEO Headquarters intervention required |

---

# 16. Escalation Rules

A dependency must be escalated when:

- A material deadline is threatened.
- Required information is unavailable.
- One department is blocking another department.
- Material financial impact is identified.
- Customer impact becomes significant.
- Product quality is materially affected.
- Supply disruption threatens operations.
- Legal or compliance exposure arises.
- Cybersecurity risk becomes material.
- Company strategic objectives are threatened.

---

# 17. CEO HQ Escalation Package

The responsible department must provide:

1. Problem statement
2. Root cause
3. Business impact
4. Customer impact
5. Financial impact
6. Available options
7. Recommended action
8. Required resources
9. Revised deadline
10. Risk assessment
11. Decision required
12. Success criteria

CEO Headquarters will determine the final executive action where departmental resolution is insufficient.
