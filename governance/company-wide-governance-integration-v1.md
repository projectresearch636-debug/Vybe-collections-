# Company-Wide Governance Integration v1

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the company-wide governance integration framework for Vybe Collections.

The purpose is to connect the approved department baselines into one coordinated operating system under CEO Headquarters.

This framework defines how departments coordinate, how company-wide priorities are established, how KPIs are consolidated, how risks are escalated, and how executive decisions are monitored.

---

## 2. Strategic Objective

Vybe Collections shall operate as one integrated organization rather than as independent departments.

Company-wide governance shall align:

**Strategy → Departments → Objectives → KPIs → Resources → Execution → Measurement → Review → Corrective Action**

---

## 3. Executive Authority

CEO Headquarters is responsible for:

- Company-wide strategic direction
- Priority setting
- Cross-department coordination
- Resource allocation
- Executive decisions
- KPI oversight
- Risk oversight
- Conflict resolution
- Governance enforcement
- Strategic review

Department teams remain responsible for execution within their approved areas of responsibility.

---

## 4. Governance Principles

Company-wide decisions shall be:

- Evidence-based
- Customer-focused
- Financially responsible
- Brand-consistent
- Operationally feasible
- Scalable
- Risk-aware
- Legally compliant
- Sustainability-conscious where practical

No material decision should rely on unsupported assumptions.

## 8. KPI Governance

Each department shall maintain measurable KPIs.

Every KPI should have:

- KPI name
- Definition
- Owner
- Department
- Measurement method
- Data source
- Reporting frequency
- Baseline
- Target
- Current status
- Variance
- Corrective action where required

No KPI values shall be fabricated.

---

## 9. KPI Ownership

Each KPI must have one accountable owner.

Supporting departments may contribute data or execution, but accountability should remain clearly assigned.

KPI ownership shall prevent:

- Duplicate ownership
- Unclear accountability
- Unmeasured objectives
- Conflicting targets

---

## 14. Cross-Department Dependencies

Departments shall identify dependencies before executing material initiatives.

Examples include:

**Product Development → Sourcing → Manufacturing → Quality → Supply Chain → Sales → Marketing → E-commerce → Customer Service**

And:

**Market Research → Brand Strategy → Product Development → Marketing → Sales → Retail/International Expansion**

And:

**Finance → Procurement → Manufacturing → Supply Chain → Sales → Marketing → Expansion**

---

## 15. Dependency Ownership

Every material cross-functional dependency should identify:

- Requesting department
- Supporting department
- Deliverable
- Owner
- Deadline
- Dependency status
- Escalation path

## 16. Cross-Department Conflict Resolution

When departments disagree, resolution should consider:

1. Company strategy
2. Customer impact
3. Financial impact
4. Operational feasibility
5. Risk
6. Legal requirements
7. Brand impact
8. Long-term value

CEO Headquarters has final executive authority where conflicts cannot be resolved at department level.

- Customer value

## 21. Risk Rating

Risks should be evaluated using:

**Likelihood × Impact**

Suggested classifications:

- Low
- Moderate
- High
- Critical

High and critical risks require defined mitigation and executive visibility.

---

## 22. Risk Ownership

Every material risk should have:

- Risk owner
- Risk description
- Likelihood
- Impact
- Current controls
- Mitigation plan
- Target resolution date
- Status

---

## 23. Escalation Framework

Issues should escalate when they exceed departmental authority or materially affect company objectives.

Escalation triggers may include:

- Material financial loss
- Major customer impact
- Significant quality failure
- Major supply interruption
- Legal or compliance exposure
- Critical technology failure
- Serious reputation risk
- Strategic objective failure

## 51. Success Criteria

Company-wide governance integration will be considered operational when:

- Every department has defined KPI ownership.
- Company-wide KPIs are consolidated.
- Material risks have assigned owners.
- Cross-department dependencies are documented.
- Executive decisions are recorded.
- Major initiatives have owners and success criteria.
- Reporting cadence is established.
- Company objectives are connected to department execution.
- Financial and operational performance can be reviewed together.
