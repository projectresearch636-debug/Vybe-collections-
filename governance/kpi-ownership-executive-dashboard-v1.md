# KPI Ownership & Executive Dashboard v1

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the company-wide KPI ownership, measurement, reporting, escalation, and executive-dashboard framework for Vybe Collections.

The objective is to convert the approved department baselines and company-wide governance framework into a measurable management system.

---

## 2. Objectives

The framework shall:

- Establish one accountable owner for every material KPI.
- Standardize KPI definitions.
- Identify authoritative data sources.
- Establish reporting frequency.
- Separate baseline, target, and actual performance.
- Provide executive visibility.
- Identify performance variance.
- Establish escalation rules.
- Support corrective action.
- Connect department KPIs to company objectives.

---

## 3. KPI Governance Model

Every material KPI should contain:

- KPI ID
- KPI name
- Department
- KPI owner
- Definition
- Calculation method
- Unit
- Data source
- Reporting frequency
- Baseline
- Target
- Actual
- Variance
- Status
- Corrective action
- Escalation requirement

No KPI value shall be fabricated.

---

## 4. KPI Ownership Principle

Every KPI must have one accountable owner.

Supporting departments may contribute data or execution, but accountability must remain unambiguous.

---

## 5. KPI Status Framework

### Green — On Target

Performance is meeting or exceeding the approved target.

### Yellow — At Risk

Performance is below target or trending toward an unacceptable variance.

### Red — Off Target

Performance materially misses the approved target or requires executive intervention.

### White — Not Measured

No verified measurement is currently available.

## 8. KPI Definition Standard

Every KPI definition must specify:

- What is measured
- Why it is measured
- How it is calculated
- Which data is included
- Which data is excluded
- Measurement period
- Responsible owner

---

## 9. Data Source Governance

Every KPI should have an identified authoritative data source.

Potential sources include:

- Finance systems
- ERP
- E-commerce platform
- Website analytics
- CRM
- Customer-service system
- Manufacturing systems
- Quality records
- Procurement records
- Supply-chain systems
- HR systems
- Marketing platforms

---

## 10. Data Quality

KPI data should be evaluated for:

- Accuracy
- Completeness
- Timeliness
- Consistency
- Traceability

Material KPI decisions should use verified data.

---

## 11. Baseline Governance

A KPI baseline shall represent an approved historical or current measurement period.

Baselines should identify:

- Measurement period
- Data source
- Calculation method
- Approval status

---

## 12. Target Governance

Targets should be:

- Specific
- Measurable
- Time-bound
- Strategically aligned
- Financially feasible
- Operationally achievable

Targets require appropriate approval.

---

## 13. Variance

Variance should compare actual performance against the approved target.

Depending on KPI type, variance may be expressed as:

- Absolute difference
- Percentage difference
- Rate difference
- Trend

The calculation method must be documented.

---

## 14. KPI Reporting Frequency

### Weekly

### Monthly

Financial, commercial, customer, workforce, and department KPIs.

### Quarterly

Strategic KPIs and company OKRs.

## 17. Escalation Rules

A KPI should be escalated when:

- It becomes Red.
- It remains Yellow for the defined escalation period.
- A material trend indicates future failure.
- The variance creates significant financial impact.
- Customer impact becomes material.
- Operational continuity is threatened.
- Legal or compliance risk emerges.

---

## 18. Corrective Action

Corrective actions should include:

- Problem
- Root cause
- Action
- Owner
- Deadline
- Expected result
- Verification method

## 20. KPI Ownership Review

KPI ownership should be reviewed when:

- Organizational responsibilities change.
- Processes change.
- Data sources change.
- KPIs are replaced.
- New strategic priorities are introduced.

---

## 21. KPI Change Control

Changes to KPI definitions, ownership, targets, or calculation methods should be documented and approved.

Historical KPI definitions should remain traceable.

---

## 22. Company OKR Integration

Each major company Objective should connect to measurable Key Results.

The hierarchy is:

**Company Objective → Key Result → KPI → Department Owner → Initiative → Action**

---

## 23. Department Alignment

Department KPIs should contribute to company-wide objectives.

Departments should not optimize KPIs in ways that materially damage company-wide performance.

---

## 24. Cross-Functional KPIs

Where a KPI depends on multiple departments:

- One department remains accountable.
- Supporting departments are identified.
- Data responsibilities are assigned.
- Dependency risks are documented.

---

## 25. KPI Conflicts

If department KPIs create conflicting incentives, CEO Headquarters shall resolve the conflict using:

- Company strategy
- Customer impact
- Financial impact
- Operational feasibility
- Risk
- Long-term value

---

## 26. Dashboard Access

KPI information should be accessible according to role and responsibility.

Sensitive financial, employee, customer, or commercially confidential information must be appropriately restricted.

---

## 27. Auditability

Every material KPI should be traceable to:

**Source Data → Calculation → Report → Decision**

---

## 28. Data Security

KPI systems should maintain:

- Access controls
- Authentication
- Appropriate permissions
- Audit trails
- Backup
- Data protection

---

## 29. Automation

Where practical, KPI collection and reporting may be automated.

Automation should be validated for:

- Accuracy
- Reliability
- Security
- Privacy
- Cost
- Business continuity

---

## 30. AI-Assisted Analytics

AI may support:

- Trend detection
- Anomaly detection
- Forecasting
- Root-cause analysis
- KPI summaries
- Risk identification

AI-generated conclusions should be reviewed appropriately before material executive decisions.

---

## 31. KPI Governance Roles

### CEO Headquarters

- Approves company-wide KPI priorities.
- Reviews executive dashboard.
- Resolves KPI conflicts.
- Escalates material performance issues.

### Department Leaders

- Own department KPIs.
- Ensure data quality.
- Execute corrective actions.
- Report performance.

### Data Analytics & Business Intelligence

- Supports measurement definitions.
- Maintains analytics consistency.
- Supports dashboard development.
- Validates data quality.

### Finance & Accounting

- Owns or validates financial measurements.
- Supports financial KPI definitions.
- Provides financial reporting.

---

## 32. KPI Maturity Roadmap

### Level 1 — Defined

KPI exists with a clear definition.

### Level 2 — Owned

KPI has an accountable owner.

### Level 3 — Measured

Reliable data is available.

### Level 4 — Managed

Target, variance, and corrective actions are established.

### Level 5 — Optimized

KPI trends support forecasting and strategic optimization.

---

## 33. Success Criteria

This framework will be considered operational when:

- Material KPIs have owners.
- KPI definitions are standardized.
- Data sources are identified.
- Baselines are established.
- Targets are approved.
- Dashboard reporting is functioning.
- Red and Yellow escalation rules are active.
- Corrective actions are tracked.
- KPI performance is integrated with company OKRs.

---

## 34. No Fabricated Data

This document establishes the KPI governance structure.

It does not claim that actual KPI values, owners, targets, or data integrations are already operational.

All actual values must be populated from verified company information.
