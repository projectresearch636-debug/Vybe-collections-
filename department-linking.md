## 1. PURPOSE

This document establishes the official relationship between all departments of Vybe Collections.

The purpose is to ensure that every department operates as part of one integrated company rather than as an isolated function.

This framework defines:

- Department mandates
- Department responsibilities
- Executive accountability
- Cross-department dependencies
- Information flows
- Required inputs
- Required outputs
- KPI ownership
- Decision and escalation paths
- Risk ownership
- Company-wide coordination

This document is the central reference for department alignment.

# 3. EXECUTIVE GOVERNANCE

CEO Headquarters coordinates all departments.

The CEO Headquarters is responsible for:

- Company vision
- Company mission
- Strategic planning
- Capital allocation
- Department prioritization
- Cross-department conflict resolution
- Company-wide KPIs
- Risk management
- Major investment decisions
- Expansion decisions
- Strategic approvals

Departments remain responsible for executing their specialized functions.

No department operates independently when a decision materially affects another department.

---

# 4. DEPARTMENT LINKING PRINCIPLES

Every department must:

1. Maintain a clearly defined mandate.
2. Maintain measurable KPIs.
3. Identify required inputs.
4. Identify expected outputs.
5. Identify upstream dependencies.
6. Identify downstream dependencies.
7. Report material risks.
8. Escalate decisions outside its authority.
9. Share relevant information with affected departments.
10. Align departmental objectives with company objectives.

# 24. CROSS-DEPARTMENT DEPENDENCY RULE

When a department proposes an initiative that materially affects another department, the affected department must be consulted before execution.

Examples:

- Product changes → D03 + D04 + D05 + D06
- Price changes → D09 + D10 + D11
- New supplier → D06 + D07 + D09 + D15
- Website changes → D12 + D16 + D17
- New market → D01 + D09 + D15 + D18
- Major hiring → D14 + affected department + D09
- AI/automation deployment → D16 + affected department
