## 4. Escalation Rules

### Red
Immediate CEO escalation when an issue threatens cash, legal compliance, customer safety, major quality, critical delivery, or a strategic gate.

### Amber
Department owner must provide corrective action and measurable recovery date.

### Green
Maintain cadence and monitor.

### Blue/Hold
No execution expansion until required evidence or gate criteria are satisfied.

## 5. Department Accountability Standard

Each active action must contain:
- One accountable owner.
- One measurable success metric.
- One due date.
- Required dependencies.
- Current status.
- Escalation condition.
- Evidence of completion.

## 6. CEO Review Protocol

At each weekly review:
1. Score prior commitments.
2. Identify missed actions.
3. Determine root cause.
4. Resolve dependencies.
5. Escalate red items.
6. Approve/reject/hold requested changes.
7. Update KPI trajectory.
8. Set next commitments.
