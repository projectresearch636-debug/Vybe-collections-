# CEO Headquarters — Textile & Fabric Sourcing Department Control Layer

**Company:** Vybe Collections

**Reporting Authority:** CEO Headquarters  
**External System:** GitHub  
**Status:** CONTROLLED / ACTIVE  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-08-30

## 1. Purpose

Establish the executive control boundary for textile and fabric sourcing, material evaluation, supplier coordination, and sourcing intelligence.

## 2. Authority

CEO Headquarters retains final authority over material strategy, major sourcing commitments, budgets, supplier risk decisions, and material investments.

Textile & Fabric Sourcing is responsible for specialist sourcing execution and material evaluation.

## 3. Scope

Approved coordination areas include:

- Fabric and textile research
- Material specifications
- Supplier discovery and evaluation
- Sampling coordination
- Material quality requirements
- Cost and lead-time analysis
- Sustainability attributes and certifications
- Sourcing risk reporting

## 4. Protection Rules

This control layer does NOT authorize:

- supplier purchase orders;
- binding commercial commitments;
- supplier credentials or secrets;
- confidential supplier contracts;
- real payment information;
- production material approvals;
- disclosure of confidential pricing;
- production changes without separate authorization.

## 5. Controlled Validation

Validation must use synthetic, non-production sourcing information only.

Required sequence:

1. Establish the control layer.
2. Create a harmless synthetic sourcing validation artifact.
3. Independently read back the artifact.
4. Record commit and verification evidence.
5. Mark the department VERIFIED only after evidence exists.

## 6. Sourcing Standards

Material evaluations should distinguish verified specifications, supplier claims, test results, estimates, and assumptions.

Where sustainability or certification claims are material, the source and verification status should be documented.
