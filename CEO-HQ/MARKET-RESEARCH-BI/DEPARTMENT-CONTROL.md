# CEO Headquarters — Market Research & Business Intelligence Department Control Layer

**Company:** Vybe Collections  
**Department:** Market Research & Business Intelligence  
**Reporting Authority:** CEO Headquarters  
**External System:** GitHub  
**Status:** CONTROLLED / ACTIVE  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-08-30

## 1. Purpose

Establish the executive control boundary for market research and business intelligence, providing a safe and auditable foundation for research, competitive intelligence, market analysis, and strategic reporting.

## 2. Authority

CEO Headquarters retains final authority over strategic priorities and material business decisions.

Market Research & Business Intelligence is responsible for research execution, evidence gathering, analysis, interpretation, and decision support.

## 3. Scope

Approved coordination areas include:

- Market sizing and segmentation
- Customer and consumer research
- Competitor intelligence
- Trend analysis
- Pricing and category analysis
- Channel and geographic intelligence
- Strategic research reporting
- Evidence-based business recommendations

## 4. Data Governance

Research outputs must distinguish facts, estimates, assumptions, and recommendations.

Confidential customer information, credentials, secrets, and restricted third-party data must not be committed to GitHub.

Sources and dates should be recorded for material research claims where applicable.

## 5. Controlled Validation

Validation must use synthetic, non-confidential research information only.

Required sequence:

1. Establish the control layer.
2. Create a harmless synthetic validation artifact.
3. Independently read back the artifact.
4. Record commit and verification evidence.
5. Mark the department VERIFIED only after evidence exists.

## 6. Quality Standards

Research should be evaluated for:

- Source reliability
- Recency
- Sample limitations
- Methodology
- Bias
- Statistical uncertainty where applicable
- Competitive comparability
- Strategic relevance

No unsupported market figures or fabricated sources may be presented as facts.
