## 3. Critical Risk Controls

### R01 — Working Capital
**Trigger:** Inventory materially exceeds approved threshold or cash forecast deteriorates.  
**Response:** Freeze non-essential purchasing, review aged stock, revise reorder points and escalate to CEO/Finance.

### R02 — Quality
**Trigger:** Defect/return trend exceeds approved threshold.  
**Response:** Contain affected production, identify root cause, implement corrective action and verify effectiveness before scaling.

### R03 — Fulfillment
**Trigger:** OTIF falls below approved service level.  
**Response:** Analyze exception cause, assign corrective owner and monitor recovery daily until stabilized.

### R07 — Compliance
**Trigger:** Potential breach, missed filing, labeling issue, contract concern or other material compliance uncertainty.  
**Response:** Escalate to Legal/Compliance; pause affected activity where necessary until reviewed.

### R09 — Technology
**Trigger:** Material system outage, unauthorized access indication, data integrity issue or critical automation failure.  
**Response:** Activate incident procedure, contain impact, preserve evidence, restore service and review root cause.

## 4. Business Continuity Priorities

- Cash and financial controls

- Quality release
- Data/KPI availability

### Tier 2 — Important
- Marketing operations

### Tier 3 — Deferrable
- Experimental initiatives
- Expansion projects without committed revenue
- Non-critical optimization work

## 5. Business Continuity Response Sequence

**Detect → Assess → Contain → Escalate → Communicate → Recover → Verify → Prevent Recurrence**

No business-continuity response should create a larger legal, safety, financial or reputation risk than the incident itself.

## 6. Compliance Gate Framework

Before material launch, expansion or regulated activity, verify as applicable:

- Corporate/legal requirements
- Product and textile labeling
- Consumer protection requirements
- Advertising/marketing claims
- Tax/accounting requirements
- Employment requirements
- Supplier/contract terms
- Data/privacy requirements
- Import/export requirements for relevant markets
- Intellectual property considerations

Specific requirements must be validated with qualified professionals for the applicable jurisdiction and activity.

## 8. Risk Review Cadence

- **Weekly:** Critical/high operational risks and open incidents.
- **Monthly:** Full enterprise risk register.
- **Quarterly:** Strategic, expansion and capital-allocation risks.
- **Event-driven:** Immediate review following material incidents or major changes.
