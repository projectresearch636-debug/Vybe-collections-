# CEO Headquarters — Fashion Design & Product Development Department Control Layer

**Company:** Vybe Collections  
**Department:** Fashion Design & Product Development  
**Reporting Authority:** CEO Headquarters  
**External System:** GitHub  
**Status:** CONTROLLED / ACTIVE  
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Purpose

Establish the executive control boundary for fashion design, product development, technical product documentation, sampling coordination, and product lifecycle workflows.

## 2. Authority

CEO Headquarters retains final authority over strategic product priorities, major investments, commercial direction, and material product decisions.

Fashion Design & Product Development is responsible for specialist design and development execution.

## 3. Scope

Approved coordination areas include:

- Collection planning
- Product concepts
- Design development
- Technical specifications
- Sample development coordination
- Fit and construction development
- Product lifecycle documentation
- Product development reporting

## 4. Protection Rules

This control layer does NOT authorize:

- publication of unreleased designs;
- disclosure of proprietary designs or patterns;
- production changes;
- supplier commitments;
- purchase orders;
- confidential customer data storage;
- credentials or secrets in GitHub;
- commercial launch decisions without separate approval.

## 5. Controlled Validation

Validation must use synthetic/non-production product information only.

Required sequence:

1. Establish the control layer.
2. Create a harmless synthetic product-development validation artifact.
3. Independently read back the artifact.
4. Record commit and verification evidence.
5. Mark the department VERIFIED only after evidence exists.

## 6. Quality Standards

Product-development records should clearly distinguish concepts, approved designs, technical specifications, samples, revisions, and production-ready documentation.

Material changes must be traceable through version history.
