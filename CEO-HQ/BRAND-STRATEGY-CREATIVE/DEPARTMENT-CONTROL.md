# Vybe Collections — CEO Headquarters
## Brand Strategy & Creative Department Control Layer

**Reporting Authority:** CEO Headquarters  
**Status:** CONTROLLED / ACTIVE  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-08-30

### Purpose
Establish the executive control boundary for brand strategy, creative direction, identity governance, campaign concepts, and brand communications.

### Authority
CEO Headquarters retains final authority over brand positioning, strategic identity decisions, major campaigns, reputational risk, and material creative investments.

Brand Strategy & Creative is responsible for specialist brand and creative execution.

### Scope
- Brand positioning and architecture
- Creative direction
- Visual identity governance
- Campaign concepts
- Brand messaging
- Creative standards and guidelines
- Brand consistency reviews
- Creative performance reporting

### Protection Rules
This control layer does NOT authorize publication of unreleased creative, disclosure of proprietary intellectual property, live campaign launches, commercial commitments, credential/secret storage, or production changes without separate approval.

### Controlled Validation
Validation must use synthetic, non-production creative information only.

1. Establish the control layer.
2. Create a harmless synthetic creative validation artifact.
3. Independently read back the artifact.
4. Record commit and verification evidence.
5. Mark VERIFIED only after evidence exists.

### Quality Standards
Creative records should distinguish concepts, drafts, approved assets, published assets, and revisions. Material brand decisions must remain traceable.
