# CEO Headquarters — Data Analytics & Business Intelligence Department Control Layer

**Company:** Vybe Collections  
**Department:** Data Analytics & Business Intelligence  
**Reporting Authority:** CEO Headquarters  
**External System:** GitHub  
**Status:** CONTROLLED / ACTIVE  
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Purpose

Establish the executive control boundary for enterprise analytics, KPI measurement, reporting, and decision-support data workflows.

## 2. Authority

CEO Headquarters retains final authority over company-wide KPI definitions, strategic targets, resource allocation, and material business decisions.

Data Analytics & Business Intelligence is responsible for data preparation, analysis, dashboard logic, metric governance, and analytical reporting.

## 3. Scope

Approved coordination areas include:

- Executive KPI reporting
- Revenue and margin analytics
- Sales analytics
- Inventory analytics
- Manufacturing and quality analytics
- Customer analytics
- Marketing performance analytics
- Operational dashboards
- Forecasting and scenario analysis
- Data-quality monitoring

## 4. Data Governance

Analytics outputs must distinguish source data, calculated metrics, assumptions, estimates, and forecasts.

Personal, confidential, credential, secret, or restricted data must not be committed to GitHub unless a separately authorized secure process explicitly permits it.

No fabricated KPI values may be presented as actual company performance.

## 5. Controlled Validation

Validation must use synthetic/non-production data only.

Required sequence:

1. Establish the control layer.
2. Create a harmless synthetic analytics validation artifact.
3. Independently read back the artifact.
4. Record commit and verification evidence.
5. Mark the department VERIFIED only after evidence exists.

## 6. KPI Governance

The department should support authoritative definitions and calculations for company metrics including revenue, gross margin, net profit, cash position, inventory turnover, defect rate, customer satisfaction, retention, website conversion, marketing ROI, sales growth, employee productivity, on-time delivery, and return rate.

When source data is unavailable, the metric must be reported as unavailable rather than invented.
