> CONTROL NOTICE: All records in this model are synthetic examples. They are not real prospects, customers, orders, revenue, forecasts, or financial results.

## 1. Objective

Establish the commercial operating pipeline connecting prospect acquisition to revenue, margin and retention, while preserving a clean transition to real business records.

## 2. Commercial Funnel

**Prospect → Qualified Lead → Opportunity → Proposal/Quote → Negotiation → Won Customer → Order → Revenue → Repeat Purchase → Retention**

## 3. Required Record Fields

| Stage | Minimum Information |
|---|---|
| Prospect | Source, date, contact identifier, segment, owner, consent/status where applicable |
| Qualified Lead | Qualification criteria, need, estimated value, owner, next action |
| Opportunity | Product/service interest, value, probability, expected close, next step |
| Proposal | Quote value, terms, expiry, decision maker, follow-up date |
| Won Customer | Customer ID, acquisition source, first-order value, date |
| Order | Order ID, SKU, units, selling price, discounts, taxes/shipping as applicable |
| Revenue | Recognized amount, period, channel, customer/order linkage |
| Margin | Revenue, COGS and contribution calculation under approved Finance definitions |
| Retention | Repeat order, time-to-repeat, retention/cohort measure |

## 5. Funnel KPIs

### Acquisition
- Prospect volume
- Cost per lead where measurable
- Source/channel mix

### Qualification
- Lead qualification rate
- Lead-to-opportunity conversion
- Sales response time

### Sales
- Opportunity-to-win rate
- Average deal/order value
- Sales cycle length
- Pipeline coverage

### Commercial Economics
- Revenue
- Gross margin
- Contribution margin
- CAC where measurable
- ROAS for attributable paid acquisition

### Customer
- First-to-second purchase rate
- Repeat purchase rate
- Return rate
- Customer satisfaction
- Retention/cohort performance

## 6. Demo Pipeline Rules

1. Every open opportunity must have an owner.
2. Every opportunity must have a next action and date.
3. Stale opportunities are flagged for review.
4. Pipeline probability must not be treated as revenue.
5. Revenue is recognized only according to the approved Finance definition.
6. Cancelled/refunded/returned transactions must be handled consistently.
7. Duplicate prospects/customers must be identified before KPI reporting.
8. Personal data must be handled according to applicable privacy and security requirements.

## 7. CEO Pipeline Review

Weekly CEO review answers:

1. How many new prospects entered?
2. Which sources produce qualified opportunities?
3. Where is funnel leakage occurring?
4. Which opportunities are at risk?
5. What revenue is committed versus merely projected?
6. What is the expected gross/contribution margin?
7. What operational capacity is required if opportunities close?
8. What cash/inventory impact follows?
9. Which decision or escalation is required?

## 9. Actual-Data Cutover

When real prospects arrive:

**Raw Prospect → Validated Prospect → Qualified → Opportunity → Customer/Order → Reconciled Revenue → Verified Actual KPI**

Real data must be kept separate from the demo dataset. No demo prospect is to be presented as a real commercial lead.

## 10. CEO Scaling Gates

Acquisition or sales expansion requires review of:
- Funnel conversion
- CAC/ROAS where applicable
- Contribution economics
- Inventory/capacity
- Fulfillment capability
- Return/quality exposure
- Cash requirements

A strong pipeline alone does not authorize production expansion or capital spending.
