## Purpose
Establish the executive control boundary for marketing strategy, campaign coordination, advertising, customer acquisition, brand communications, and marketing performance reporting.

## Authority
CEO Headquarters retains final authority over strategic marketing priorities, major campaign budgets, brand-risk decisions, material advertising commitments, and company-wide growth priorities.

Marketing & Advertising is responsible for specialist campaign planning, execution, measurement, and optimization.

## Scope
- Marketing planning
- Campaign coordination
- Advertising operations
- Customer acquisition
- Content and promotional coordination
- Audience strategy
- Campaign performance analysis
- Marketing ROI reporting

## Protection Rules
This control layer does NOT authorize live campaign launches, advertising-account changes, payment-method changes, customer-audience uploads, confidential customer-data exposure, or material budget commitments without separate approval.

## Controlled Validation
Validation must use synthetic, non-production marketing information only.

1. Establish the control layer.
2. Create a harmless synthetic marketing validation artifact.
3. Independently read back the artifact.
4. Record commit and verification evidence.
5. Mark VERIFIED only after evidence exists.

## KPI Standards
Where source data exists, support customer acquisition cost, conversion rate, return on ad spend, marketing ROI, traffic, engagement, retention, campaign revenue, and funnel performance.

Unavailable values must be reported as unavailable rather than invented.
