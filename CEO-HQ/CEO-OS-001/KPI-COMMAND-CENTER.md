## Executive Objective
Create one company-wide KPI architecture. Actual values must come from approved source systems; unavailable values are marked DATA REQUIRED.

## KPI Governance
1. Every KPI has an accountable owner.
2. Every KPI has a precise definition and unit.
3. Every actual has a source and reporting period.
4. Estimates and forecasts are labeled separately.
5. Missing data is never replaced with invented values.
6. Material KPI changes remain traceable.

## Department KPI Ownership

- Market Research & BI: market size, segment growth, competitor/trend intelligence.
- Brand Strategy & Creative: brand consistency, creative throughput, brand performance.
- Fashion Design & Product Development: development cycle time, sample iteration, launch readiness.
- Textile & Fabric Sourcing: material cost, lead time, supplier quality, certification verification.
- Manufacturing & Production: output, efficiency, capacity, production lead time.
- Quality Assurance: defect rate, first-pass yield, corrective-action closure.
- Procurement & Vendor Management: purchase-price variance, supplier OTIF, vendor risk.
- Supply Chain & Logistics: inventory turnover, fulfillment cycle time, on-time delivery.
- Finance & Accounting: revenue, gross margin, net profit, cash, budget variance.
- Sales & Business Development: pipeline, win rate, sales growth, forecast accuracy.
- Marketing & Advertising: CAC, ROAS, marketing ROI, funnel performance.
- E-commerce & Website: conversion, AOV, cart abandonment, uptime/performance.
- Customer Service: CSAT, response time, resolution time, retention, complaints.
- Human Resources: headcount, hiring time, retention/turnover, engagement.
- Legal & Compliance: compliance status, contract/risk tracking, deadlines.
- IT, AI & Automation: availability, automation reliability, integration health.
- Data Analytics & BI: data quality, dashboard freshness, metric governance.
- Retail & International Expansion: store/channel revenue, contribution margin, payback, geographic growth.
