## Purpose
Establish the executive control boundary for sales operations, business development, pipeline management, partnerships, revenue growth, and commercial reporting.

## Authority
CEO Headquarters retains final authority over strategic revenue targets, major commercial commitments, pricing policy, strategic partnerships, and material commercial risk.

Sales & Business Development is responsible for specialist sales execution, pipeline management, opportunity development, and commercial reporting.

## Scope
- Sales pipeline coordination
- Lead and opportunity management
- Business development
- Partnership development
- Sales forecasting
- Commercial reporting
- Account development
- Revenue performance analysis

## Protection Rules
This control layer does NOT authorize contracts, binding commercial commitments, pricing exceptions, payments, customer-data exposure, sales-system production changes, or disclosure of confidential commercial information without separate approval.

## Controlled Validation
Validation must use synthetic, non-production sales information only.

1. Establish the control layer.
2. Create a harmless synthetic sales validation artifact.
3. Independently read back the artifact.
4. Record commit and verification evidence.
5. Mark VERIFIED only after evidence exists.

## KPI Standards
Where source data exists, support revenue, sales growth, pipeline value, conversion rate, average order value, win rate, sales-cycle length, customer acquisition cost, retention, and forecast accuracy.

Unavailable values must be reported as unavailable rather than invented.
