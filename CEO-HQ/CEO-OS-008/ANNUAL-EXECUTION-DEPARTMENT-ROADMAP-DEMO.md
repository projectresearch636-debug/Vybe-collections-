## 4. Weekly CEO Operating Cycle

**Monday:** KPI and risk review.  
**Tuesday:** Department exceptions and blockers.  
**Wednesday:** Cross-functional corrective actions.  
**Thursday:** Strategic initiative progress.  
**Friday:** CEO decisions, accountability and next-week priorities.

## 5. Monthly Management Cycle

1. Close and validate financial data.
2. Score company KPIs.
3. Review departmental performance.
4. Review strategic initiatives.
5. Reallocate resources where justified.
6. Update risk register.
7. Confirm next-month priorities.

## 6. Executive Gates

### Gate A — Data Readiness
No official target without verified baseline data.

### Gate B — Product/Channel Economics
No scaling decision without SKU/channel economics.

### Gate C — Operational Readiness
No major volume increase without manufacturing, quality, inventory and logistics capacity.

### Gate D — Capital Approval
No major spending without quantified business case and cash-impact review.

### Gate E — Expansion
No retail/international launch without market evidence, compliance, operational readiness and return/payback case.

## 8. Accountability Rule

Every active initiative must have one accountable owner, one measurable success metric, one deadline, identified dependencies, and an escalation path. A department may collaborate broadly, but accountability cannot be shared ambiguously.
