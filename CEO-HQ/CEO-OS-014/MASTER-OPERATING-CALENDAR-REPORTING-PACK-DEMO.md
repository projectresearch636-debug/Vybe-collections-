# Vybe Collections — CEO-OS-014
## Master Operating Calendar & Reporting Pack

**Status:** ACTIVE / DEMO-READY OPERATING STANDARD
**Date:** 2026-08-30
**Authority:** CEO Headquarters

> CONTROL NOTICE: This is an operating governance template. Demo values remain synthetic and must not be treated as actual company performance.

## 1. Executive Objective

Create a recurring management rhythm so every department reports consistently, exceptions are escalated promptly, and CEO decisions are recorded and followed through.

## 2. Operating Calendar

### Daily — Department Operations
- Production: output, downtime, defects, urgent constraints.
- Supply Chain/Logistics: orders, dispatches, delivery exceptions.
- Sales: pipeline movement, urgent opportunities, blockers.
- Customer Service: tickets, escalations, returns.
- E-commerce/Marketing: traffic, conversion, campaign anomalies.
- Finance: cash-critical movements and exceptions.
- IT: critical incidents and automation failures.

**CEO escalation:** immediate for critical cash, legal/compliance, safety, customer, security or business-continuity issues.

### Weekly — CEO Operating Review
**Monday:** KPI pack issued and data quality checked.
**Tuesday:** Department exception reports.
**Wednesday:** Cross-functional blocker resolution.
**Thursday:** Strategic initiative and OKR review.
**Friday:** CEO decisions, action ownership and next-week priorities.

### Monthly — Business Review
- Financial close and management P&L.
- Revenue, margin and cash review.
- Inventory and working capital.
- Product/SKU economics.
- Sales pipeline and marketing economics.
- Quality, production and fulfillment.
- Customer service/returns.
- Workforce and technology.
- Risk register.
- Resource allocation decisions.

### Quarterly — Strategy Review
- OKR scoring.
- Strategic pillar performance.
- Market/competitive intelligence.
- Product portfolio decisions.
- Capacity and supplier strategy.
- Capital allocation.
- Expansion readiness.
- Risk appetite and mitigation.

### Annual — Company Planning
- Strategy refresh.
- Annual operating plan.
- Budget and capital plan.
- Organization/capacity plan.
- Product roadmap.
- Market/channel roadmap.
- Risk and continuity review.

## 3. Standard Department Report

Every department submission must contain:

1. Executive summary.
2. KPI actual/target/status.
3. Progress against commitments.
4. Completed actions with evidence.
5. Missed commitments and root cause.
6. Risks/issues.
7. Cross-department dependencies.
8. Decisions required.
9. Next-period priorities.
10. Data quality exceptions.

## 4. CEO Weekly Pack

### Page 1 — Executive Snapshot
Revenue, gross margin, operating profit, cash, inventory, orders, conversion, ROAS, quality, OTIF, returns and CSAT.

### Page 2 — Exception Queue
All red/amber KPIs with owner, cause, recovery action and deadline.

### Page 3 — Strategic Initiatives
OKR and strategic initiative progress.

### Page 4 — Financial Control
P&L, cash, working capital, major spending and variance analysis.

### Page 5 — Operations
Production, sourcing, quality, inventory and logistics.

### Page 6 — Customer & Growth
Sales, marketing, e-commerce, customer service and retention.

### Page 7 — Risk
Critical/high risks, compliance gates and business continuity issues.

### Page 8 — CEO Decisions
Approved, rejected, held and escalated decisions with owners and due dates.

## 5. Reporting Discipline

- Use one approved KPI definition.
- State reporting period and source.
- Separate actuals, estimates and demo data.
- Never hide a missed KPI behind a blended aggregate.
- Explain material variances.
- Attach evidence for completed actions.
- Escalate blockers instead of carrying them indefinitely.

## 6. Meeting Decision Protocol

**Review → Diagnose → Decide → Assign → Record → Follow Up**

Every decision record must state:
- Decision
- Rationale
- Owner
- Due date
- Expected outcome
- Dependencies
- Review date
