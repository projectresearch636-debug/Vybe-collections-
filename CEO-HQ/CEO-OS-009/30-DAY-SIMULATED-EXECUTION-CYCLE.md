## 3. Simulated 30-Day Events

### Days 1–7 — Diagnose
- Finance flags inventory working-capital exposure.
- E-commerce identifies checkout/funnel leakage.
- Marketing reviews lower-performing campaigns.
- QA begins defect root-cause analysis.
- Logistics identifies late-delivery exception categories.

**CEO decision:** No material increase in acquisition spend until conversion and ROAS improve.

### Days 8–14 — Correct

- Supply Chain reviews slow-moving inventory and reorder points.
- Logistics establishes delivery exception monitoring.

**CEO decision:** Preserve cash and working capital while corrective actions run.

## 4. Simulated CEO Verdict

**Overall:** 🟢 Improving, but not yet ready for unrestricted scaling.

The simulation demonstrates that cross-functional intervention can improve commercial and operational KPIs without immediately increasing capital exposure.

## 5. Lessons Validated

1. KPI exceptions can trigger coordinated action.
2. Marketing should scale only after economics improve.
3. Inventory must be managed as a cash decision, not only a warehouse metric.
4. Quality and returns require Product + Manufacturing + QA + Customer Service coordination.
5. Delivery performance requires Supply Chain/Logistics visibility.
6. CEO decisions need explicit owners and measurable outcomes.
