## 4. CEO Exception Queue

### P1 — Inventory
**Issue:** Simulated inventory remains above target.
**Owner:** Supply Chain + Finance
**Action:** Aging/reorder analysis.
**Exit condition:** Inventory exposure reaches approved threshold without harming service levels.

### P1 — Quality
**Issue:** Simulated defect rate remains above target.
**Owner:** QA + Manufacturing + Product
**Action:** Root-cause and corrective-action program.
**Exit condition:** Defect rate reaches approved quality threshold.

### P1 — Fulfillment
**Issue:** Simulated OTIF remains below target.
**Owner:** Supply Chain + Logistics
**Action:** Delivery exception analysis.
**Exit condition:** OTIF reaches approved threshold.

### P1 — Returns
**Issue:** Simulated return rate remains above target.
**Owner:** Customer Service + Product + E-commerce
**Action:** Return-reason analysis and corrective changes.
**Exit condition:** Return rate reaches approved threshold.

### P2 — Conversion/ROAS
**Issue:** Simulated performance is near/above target after intervention.
**Owner:** E-commerce + Marketing + Finance
**Action:** Maintain controlled optimization.
**Exit condition:** Economics remain above gate for sustained review periods.

## 6. CEO Weekly Command Rhythm

**1. Performance:** KPI actual/simulated vs target.
**2. Exceptions:** Identify red/amber items.
**3. Accountability:** Confirm owner and due date.
**4. Dependencies:** Resolve cross-department blockers.
**5. Decisions:** Approve, reject, hold or escalate.
**6. Resources:** Reallocate only with evidence.
**7. Next cycle:** Set measurable priorities.

## 7. Decision Rules

- Never present synthetic data as actual data.
- Never approve major spending without a quantified business case.
- Never scale a bottlenecked operation without capacity evidence.
- Never optimize revenue at the expense of contribution/cash without explicit approval.
- Never launch expansion without market, compliance, operational and financial readiness.
- Every major action requires an owner and measurable success condition.

## 8. Real-Data Cutover

When actual prospects and transactions become available:

1. Preserve demo data as simulation history.
2. Load actual data into a separate production layer.
3. Validate source, period, definitions and reconciliation.
4. Mark verified actuals distinctly.
5. Recalculate company KPIs.
6. Re-score OKRs.
7. Rebuild the risk and opportunity register.
8. Review strategy and capital allocation using verified actuals.
