## 1. Strategic Intent
Build Vybe Collections into a respected, profitable, customer-centric, operationally excellent and globally competitive apparel company.

## 2. Strategic Pillars
1. Product leadership — differentiated products with disciplined SKU economics.
2. Profitable growth — prioritize contribution and cash generation, not revenue alone.
3. Operational excellence — improve sourcing, manufacturing, quality, inventory and fulfillment.
4. Customer engine — improve conversion, retention, service and repeat purchase economics.
5. Brand strength — maintain consistent positioning, creative quality and customer trust.
6. Scalable technology — use reliable data, AI and automation to improve decisions.
7. Selective expansion — enter channels/geographies only after evidence-based gates.

## 3. Three-Year Direction — Planning Model

### Year 1 — Foundation
- Establish verified actual-data baseline.
- Prove core product/channel unit economics.
- Stabilize quality, inventory and fulfillment.
- Build repeatable acquisition and retention systems.
- Establish weekly CEO operating cadence.

### Year 2 — Scale
- Scale products and channels meeting contribution-margin gates.
- Expand production and supplier capacity with quality controls.
- Improve automation and data integration.
- Evaluate selective retail and international opportunities.

### Year 3 — Expansion
- Scale proven domestic and international channels.
- Strengthen brand equity and product portfolio depth.
- Optimize capital allocation across products, channels and markets.
- Pursue sustainable long-term profitability and resilience.

## 4. Annual Planning Framework
Official annual targets must be calculated only after real baseline data is validated.

Required target categories: revenue, gross margin, contribution margin, operating profit, cash/working capital, inventory turnover, acquisition, retention, product launches, quality, fulfillment and workforce capacity.

## 5. Quarterly Strategic Gates
- Q1 — Baseline & Stabilize: validate data, unit economics, constraints and customer proposition.
- Q2 — Optimize: improve conversion, marketing economics, quality, inventory and fulfillment.
- Q3 — Scale Winners: invest only in products/channels meeting approved economic and capacity thresholds.
- Q4 — Expansion Readiness: evaluate new channels/geographies using market evidence, compliance, supply chain and payback analysis.

## 6. Capital Allocation Rules
No material investment is approved solely because revenue is growing.

Every major investment requires objective, cost, expected benefit, cash impact, capacity requirement, risks, return/payback case, owner and stop criteria.

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Growth without contribution | High | Channel/SKU unit-economics gates |
| Excess inventory | High | Aging, reorder and demand controls |
| Quality erosion during scale | High | QA and supplier/manufacturing controls |
| Marketing inefficiency | Medium/High | ROAS/CAC and contribution monitoring |
| Cash strain | High | Rolling cash forecast and investment gates |
| Expansion too early | High | Market evidence + payback + operational readiness |
| Data quality failure | High | KPI governance and source validation |
