## 1. Purpose

This registry is the central executive control map for all company departments. It defines departmental ownership, integration status, priority, and executive coordination requirements.

No department is considered technically linked merely because it is listed here. A department becomes **LINKED / VERIFIED** only after its authorized integration path and required validation evidence are successfully demonstrated.

## 2. Status Definitions

- **VERIFIED:** Authorized integration has been successfully tested and independently verified.
- **CONTROLLED:** Department control structure exists; operational integration is not yet fully verified.
- **PENDING:** Department has not yet been connected through a validated control layer.
- **BLOCKED:** A known technical, authorization, legal, financial, or operational blocker prevents progress.

## 5. Executive Governance

CEO Headquarters coordinates all departments and resolves cross-functional conflicts.

Each department remains responsible for its specialist domain. Department integration does not transfer final executive authority away from CEO Headquarters.

Major changes must be evaluated against:

- Strategic alignment
- Financial impact
- Customer impact
- Operational feasibility
- Brand consistency
- Legal and compliance requirements
- Security and technology risk
- Sustainability considerations
- Scalability

## 6. Department Integration Standard

Every future department link should follow this sequence:

1. Identify department and owner.
2. Define control boundary.
3. Define approved systems and repository path where applicable.
4. Establish minimum required permissions.
5. Execute a controlled non-production validation.
6. Independently verify the result.
7. Record evidence and commit/change identifier.
8. Mark the department VERIFIED only after evidence exists.

## 7. Risk Control

The registry must not be treated as authorization for production activity.

No department may:

- expose secrets or credentials;
- bypass approved integrations;
- modify production systems without authorization;
- make destructive changes without explicit approval;
- treat metadata or reported permissions as proof of operational capability.
