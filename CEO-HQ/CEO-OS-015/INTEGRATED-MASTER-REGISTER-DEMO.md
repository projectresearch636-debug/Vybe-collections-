## 7. CEO Master Dashboard Logic

Every reporting cycle must answer:

1. What changed?
2. Which KPI missed target?
3. Why did it happen?
4. Who owns the response?
5. What action is being taken?
6. What risk does it address?
7. What decision is required?
8. When will success be measured?
9. What evidence proves completion?
10. Should the strategy/resource allocation change?

## 8. Governance Rules

- One KPI definition per approved metric.
- One accountable owner per action.
- No action without a measurable success condition.
- No major decision without evidence or explicitly documented assumptions.
- Demo, estimate and actual values remain visibly distinct.
- Red risks and critical compliance issues cannot be silently carried forward.
- Closed actions require evidence, not verbal confirmation.
