# CEO Headquarters — Legal & Compliance Department Control Layer

**Company:** Vybe Collections  
**Department:** Legal & Compliance  
**Reporting Authority:** CEO Headquarters  
**External System:** GitHub  
**Status:** CONTROLLED / ACTIVE  
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Purpose

Establish the executive control boundary for Legal & Compliance and provide a safe, auditable foundation for future legal and regulatory workflows.

## 2. Authority

CEO Headquarters retains final executive authority over material legal, regulatory, contractual, compliance, and reputational decisions.

Legal & Compliance is the specialist function responsible for legal analysis, compliance coordination, documentation standards, and escalation.

## 3. Scope

Approved coordination areas include:

- Regulatory compliance
- Contract governance
- Intellectual property coordination
- Consumer protection requirements
- Apparel labeling and product compliance coordination
- Privacy and data-protection coordination
- Corporate records and legal documentation
- Legal risk reporting

## 4. Protection Rules

This control layer does NOT authorize:

- committing confidential legal documents;
- exposing privileged communications;
- publishing legal advice;
- making legal representations on behalf of the company;
- accepting contractual obligations;
- changing regulatory filings;
- storing personal data, credentials, or secrets in GitHub;
- production legal/compliance system changes without separate authorization.

## 5. Controlled Validation

Validation must use synthetic, non-confidential information only.

Required sequence:

1. Establish the control layer.
2. Create a harmless synthetic validation artifact.
3. Independently read back the artifact.
4. Record commit and verification evidence.
5. Mark the department VERIFIED only after evidence exists.

## 6. Risk Controls

Legal and compliance workflows must identify jurisdiction, applicable regulation, material assumptions, deadlines, and escalation requirements where relevant.

Where professional legal advice is required, the workflow must escalate to qualified legal counsel rather than treating an automated output as legal advice.
